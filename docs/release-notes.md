# marginal v0.1.0

The first release: every one of the eighteen mandatory steps of the build order, the site on
GitHub Pages, the API on Fly with Neon, and every published figure re-derived by the claim gate
before the tag. `RESULTS.md` carries the checklist with one line of evidence per item and the
limitations; `DECISIONS.md` carries the dated decisions and the two reversals.

Alderquist is a fictional direct to consumer home goods brand and its market is simulated with
known truth. The email experiment is Kevin Hillstrom's public 2008 dataset, the purchase history
is the UCI Online Retail II dataset, and the large uplift set is Criteo's public research
release. No real company's spend and no real customer's identity appears here.
